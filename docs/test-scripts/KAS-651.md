# Test script: KAS-651 Try-on v2, one-shot image edit + "photo of me wearing it"

Written by QA (KAS-654) on 25 Nov 2026 from KAS-651's outcome and its 13 acceptance checks. I also read Kasper's request form and the brief it points to (`docs/try-on-v2-brief.md` on `claude/tender-pascal-pdm1il`) for what each change is for. I read no Engineer branch, and nothing here says how to build it. In the repository this file goes in `docs/test-scripts/KAS-651.md`.

**Markers**
- `automated`: the Engineer writes a test with the name given, and it runs with the repository's tests. Outside services (the new image-edit provider, FASHN, Claude) are replaced by stand-ins. No test calls a real provider.
- `manual (test copy)`: QA runs it through the real screens on the QA preview (`agent/qa-fashun`) as QA accounts A and B only, with desktop (1920 × 1080) and phone (390 × 844) screenshots per step. **The preview runs on the live database with the live keys, so every picture made here is real spend** and counts in this month's real try-on spend.
- `live-safe`: safe on the live site after the merge. No upload, no picture, no cost, no personal data.
- `document`: QA reads a document or the PR; no screen involved (checks 1 and 13).

**Words used below**
- **New provider**: the image-edit model Kasper picks in check 1.
- **Picture**: one try-on result for one outfit.
- **Price**: the price per picture for the new provider that Kasper signs off (`TRY_ON_PRICE_USD`). The FASHN price stays US$0.075 per FASHN image (2 for a top and bottom).
- **Worn photo**: a "photo of me wearing it" on one wardrobe item.
- **Fit notes**: the short garment-only notes made from a worn photo.
- **Labels that stay word for word**: AI label "Made by AI. Fit and details may not be exact."; failure "Try-on didn't work this time. Nothing was charged. Try again later."; paused "Try-on is paused until 1 [next month]. Your saved try-ons still show."
- **Privacy note**: the new wording Kasper signs off for check 8. QA compares against that signed-off text word for word; until it exists, steps that read it can't pass.

## Open points for the Delivery Manager
1. **The spike's person photo.** M14 reuses the spike's 5 outfits, so the spike must use a computer-generated person (QA never uses a real person's photo), and the write-up in the repository shouldn't show a real person unless Kasper says so. Recommend: fictional person A.
2. **Shoes and outerwear (check 4).** "As decided per category in the plan": the decision must be written (in the write-up or the build report) before the review, or T4 and M5 have nothing to compare against.
3. **Does agreeing to the old FASHN note still count?** The note now names a different provider with different terms. Recommend: people agree again to the new note before their next upload or picture. It decides whether any QA account is "not agreed" for M1, M12 and L1. If the old agreement counts and both accounts have agreed, the "not agreed" path is automated only (T19).
4. **Fit notes when the worn photos are deleted (check 10).** They come from the person's photo. Recommend: cleared with the worn photo.
5. **Fit notes reading fails (check 11).** Recommend: keep the worn photo, no notes, no message to the person, try-on uses the photo alone.
6. **Are fit notes shown on the item page?** The brief makes it optional. Recommend shown: otherwise check 11 can't be seen by a person and QA reads them from the data the page loads.
7. **Spend across the price change.** This month already has FASHN images at US$0.075. Recommend: each picture is counted at the price it was made at, so the panel and cap stay true when the price setting or provider changes.
