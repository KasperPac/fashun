# Test script: KAS-794 try-on v2 follow-ups (photo-link life, focus after delete, Profile labels)

Written by QA (KAS-816) on 26 Nov 2026 from KAS-794's outcome and its 6 acceptance checks. I read no Engineer branch. To name today's screens and controls I read `master` at `737b6cf` (KAS-651 merged) and QA's live check of it (KAS-774). In the repository this file goes in `docs/test-scripts/KAS-794.md`.

**Markers**
- `automated`: the Engineer writes a test with the name given, and it runs with the repository's tests. Storage, Gemini and Claude are replaced by stand-ins. No test calls a real provider.
- `real-use`: QA runs it through the real screens on the QA preview (`agent/qa-fashun`) as QA accounts A and B only, with desktop (1920 × 1080) and phone (390 × 844) screenshots for each step. **The preview runs on the live database with the live keys**, so a picture made here is real spend.
- `live-safe`: safe on the live site after the merge. No upload, no picture, no cost.
- `document`: QA reads the diff or the PR; no screen involved.

**Words used below**
- **Private link**: the address the app gives the browser for one of the three try-on files: the **try-on photo** (Profile), a **worn photo** ("On you" in the item sheet), or a **saved picture** (an outfit's try-on result).
- **Link life**: how long a private link opens after the app issues it. Today it is 1 hour; after this change, 5 minutes.
- **Opens**: fetched with no cookies, in a fresh signed-out browser context, the link returns the image (HTTP 200, an image type, more than 0 bytes). **Refused**: it returns no image (HTTP 400 or 403, or an error body).
- **Shows**: the `<img>` on the page has finished loading with `naturalWidth > 0`, and the page shows no "Couldn't load your photo." or broken-image tile.
- **Left open**: the screen stays on screen, untouched, for 6 minutes (5 minutes plus a margin).
- **Garment photo**: a wardrobe item's own product photo. It is not a try-on file, and its links stay as they are (open point 2).

**Timing and clock margin.** A link must still open 4 minutes after it was issued and must be refused 5 minutes 30 seconds after. The 30 seconds allow for clock drift between the server and storage. Steps with a wait are grouped so that one 6-minute wait covers several of them.
